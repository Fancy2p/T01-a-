Television Energy Consumption

Learn about the factors that influence television electricity consumption in Australian households.

How Much Energy Does a Television Use?

Television energy consumption can vary depending on factors such as screen size, display technology, brightness settings, usage time and standby behaviour.

Screen Size

Larger televisions generally require more energy to operate than smaller models, although efficiency varies between individual products.

Display Technology

Different display technologies can have different electricity consumption characteristics.

Usage Time

A television that is used for several hours each day will generally consume more electricity over a year than one used less frequently.
